For Mac and iPad

Your text. Your syntax. Your workflow.

Pilcrow is a professional plain-text editor. Write in Markdown, AsciiDoc, reStructuredText, LaTeX, or a syntax you define yourself, then publish to HTML, PDF and Word.

Coming soon to the App Store Explore the features

Requires macOS 14 or later, or iPadOS 17 or later.

  • No account
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking
  • Works offline
  • Your files stay where you keep them
  • Available in 23 languages

The idea

An editor that adapts to your writing, not the other way round.

Most editors pick a markup language for you and a publishing path to go with it. Pilcrow keeps the three apart, so you can change any one without disturbing the others.

¶

Your text

Documents are ordinary plain-text files, saved where you choose. Open a multi-gigabyte log as easily as a short note, and read your work in any other editor, now or in ten years.

§

Your syntax

A profile tells Pilcrow what your markup means. Use CommonMark, AsciiDoc, reStructuredText or LaTeX, or write a profile for the notation you invented.

✦

Your workflow

Macros, fill-in templates, folder-wide search, version history, style review and book projects. Then publish the same source as a web page, a PDF or a Word document.

Write and preview

Type the source. Watch the page appear.

Edit shows exactly what you typed. Preview shows the finished page. Side by Side shows both at once, scrolling together by heading.

  • Syntax colouring as you type, driven by the profile, so the structure of your document is visible at a glance.
  • An outline of your headings in the sidebar, for jumping around long documents.
  • A formatting toolbar that follows the active syntax, for the moments you would rather tap than type.
  • Light and dark app themes, each with its own look for the page you are writing.

Syntax profiles

One document. Any markup.

The same heading and the same bold phrase, written four ways. Pilcrow understands all of them, and each one publishes with the same themes and the same exports.

CommonMark.md
# Field Notes

The river **rose** overnight.
AsciiDoc.adoc
= Field Notes

The river *rose* overnight.
reStructuredText.rst
Field Notes
===========

The river **rose** overnight.
LaTeX.tex
\section{Field Notes}

The river \textbf{rose} overnight.

Need something else? Write your own profile. Pilcrow includes an editor for profile rules, a way to test them, and profiles can be shared as files.

Equations, diagrams, charts

Draw it from text. Offline.

Write an equation, a flowchart or a data chart as text, right in your document. Pilcrow draws it for you, on your device, with no network connection.

  • Maths typeset with MathJax.
  • Diagrams drawn with Mermaid: flowcharts, sequences, journeys and more.
  • Charts from Vega-Lite specifications.
  • Tables, task lists and footnotes, rendered the way you expect.
  • All of it carries through to PDF, Word and image export.

Under the hood

Gigabytes, not seconds.

  • The file stays on disk. Pilcrow never reads it all into memory.
  • Your edits are kept separately, so the original is left alone until you save.
  • Only the part you can see is loaded into the editor.

Built for scale

Open the file other editors choke on.

Logs, data exports, whole manuscripts, a decade of notes in one file. Pilcrow opens multi-gigabyte documents quickly and keeps typing smooth, because it was designed around big files from the start.

Line counting and syntax analysis run in the background and the status bar tells you when they finish. You can keep working meanwhile.

Everything else

The details a serious writer notices.

Pilcrow is a full editor, not a notepad with a preview pane.

⌘

Command palette

Reach any command by typing its name, and rebind every shortcut to suit your hands.

≡

Multiple carets and columns

Edit many places at once, select a rectangle of text, and run line, case and deletion commands.

§

Macros and fill-in fields

Expand a short trigger into a date, a signature or a whole template, then Tab from field to field.

⌥

Search a whole folder

Find and replace across every file you have open, with literal text, whole words or regular expressions.

†

Version history and compare

Look back at earlier versions, take back one change, or review a colleague's copy and accept only what you want.

✓

Link checker

Find broken links and missing images before you publish, not after.

‡

Safe by default

Autosave, crash recovery and text-encoding control. Pilcrow asks before it closes a document with unsaved changes.

❦

Your own look

Choose fonts, size, spacing and backdrops. Import themes, or write one in CSS for the page itself.

∞

Translate, listen, count

Translate a selection or a whole document, have it read aloud, and keep an eye on your word count and writing statistics.

¶

Table of contents and front matter

Generate a table of contents of any depth, number your headings, and keep metadata in front matter.

✦

Style review

Check your writing against a house style. Many rules run entirely on your device, with no AI involved.

❡

Print that matches the page

Printing uses the same rendering as PDF export, so what you preview is what lands on paper.

Book projects

Write the whole book, not just a chapter.

Turn a folder of chapters and scenes into a project. Plan on cards, track progress against a word target, then compile the manuscript to HTML, PDF or Word.

  • List, synopsis and card views of your manuscript, with status, point of view and location for each scene.
  • Your files stay ordinary text. A small manifest records the order; nothing is rearranged or locked in.
  • Compile presets such as “Editor DOCX” and “Print PDF”, with project variables for the title and author.
A book projectfolder
My Novel/
├─ manifest.pilcrow
├─ front/
├─ ch01/
│  ├─ scene1.md
│  └─ scene2.md
├─ ch02/
├─ back/
└─ notes/

The manifest holds the order and details. The files are yours.

Themes

The app and the page, styled apart.

An application theme changes how Pilcrow looks while you write. A render theme changes how the finished page looks. Work in a dark app and publish on white paper, or the reverse, without a compromise.

Render themes are plain CSS, so a designer can make one in an afternoon. Import and share them as files.

A dark app around a light page.

Optional assistant

Bring your own AI, or none at all.

The assistant is switched off until you connect it. Choose the service, or run a model on your own machine, and decide what each request may include.

  • Your provider, your key. Connect OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, a custom endpoint, or local software such as Ollama or LM Studio.
  • You see what is sent. Pilcrow names the destination and asks your permission before the first request, and a preview shows exactly what will go.
  • Sensitive files stay out. Files such as .env and *.pem are excluded by default, and you can exclude more.
  • Nothing passes through us. There is no Pluria Labs server in the middle, and your key lives in the system Keychain.

What a review can do

  • Ask a question about your document, with only the context you choose.
  • Review a selection or a whole document against a style guide, one finding at a time.
  • Show the evidence and a suggested fix for each finding, and apply only the ones you agree with.
  • Run its local, rule-based checks with no AI at all.

Privacy

Your writing is none of our business.

Pilcrow is built to work without a server of ours. There is nothing to sign up for, and nothing for us to see.

No account

You never create a Pilcrow account or sign in to use any feature.

No tracking

No advertising, no analytics SDK and no telemetry service run by Pluria Labs.

Your files stay yours

Documents are saved where you choose. Pluria Labs never receives them.

Offline by design

Maths, diagrams and charts are drawn on your device. Remote images are off until you turn them on.

Nothing runs from a document

Pilcrow does not execute scripts embedded in the files you open.

Reports are your call

The diagnostic log leaves your device only if you choose to send it, and you choose what it includes.

Questions

Good to know.

What does Pilcrow run on?

Pilcrow runs on Mac (macOS 14 or later) and iPad (iPadOS 17 or later).

Where can I learn how to use Pilcrow?

The User Guide is a task-based course in short chapters, from creating your first document to building a book project, with search across the whole guide.

What is a syntax profile?

A profile is a set of rules that tells Pilcrow what your markup means: which characters make a heading, a list or emphasis, and how each becomes HTML, PDF or Word. Pilcrow includes profiles for CommonMark, AsciiDoc, reStructuredText, LaTeX and plain text, and you can write your own.

Can I open files from iCloud Drive or other cloud folders?

Yes. Pilcrow edits files in place wherever you keep them, including iCloud Drive and other storage providers your device supports. Your storage provider handles syncing; Pluria Labs never receives a copy.

Do I need an AI service to use Pilcrow?

No. The assistant is optional and switched off until you connect a service. Everything else, including the local checks in style review, works without it.

Does Pilcrow send my documents anywhere?

No. The only time text leaves your device is when you deliberately send it through the assistant to a service you chose, load a remote image you enabled, or open a link. The privacy policy lists every case.

How much does it cost?

Every feature is free. Supporting development with a subscription or voting credits is optional.

I found a bug. What should I do?

Please report it. Pilcrow can prepare a diagnostic report for you, and you choose what it contains.
