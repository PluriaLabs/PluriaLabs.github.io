Before you report a bug

  • Search the open and closed issues. If your problem is already there, add what you know to it.
  • Check that you are using the latest version of Pilcrow.
  • In Pilcrow, choose Report a Bug to start a report. It fills in your Pilcrow version for you and can add a diagnostic log, which you can read first and choose what to include.
  • Issues on GitHub are public. Look through anything you attach, and never post a password, a key or a private document.

Common questions

Where are my documents stored?

Wherever you saved them. Pilcrow edits your files in place, on your device or in a storage provider such as iCloud Drive. Pluria Labs never receives them.

Pilcrow quit while I was writing. Is my work lost?

Pilcrow keeps a temporary recovery copy of unsaved text to help you recover after a crash or interruption, and removes it once the document is saved or closed normally. Version History can also bring back an earlier saved version.

Something renders oddly in Preview.

Check, in order, that the right syntax profile is active, that your source is valid for it, and that the render theme you want is selected. The profile decides what your markup means; the theme decides how it looks.

How is my privacy protected?

Read the privacy policy. In short: no account, no ads, no analytics, and your documents stay with you.
