Install a theme

  1. Download a theme. Keep it as a zip file; if your browser unzips it, compress the folder again.
  2. In Pilcrow, open Settings ▸ Library ▸ Themes, choose Import Theme… and pick the zip file.
  3. Choose the app theme under Settings ▸ Appearance and the render theme under Settings ▸ Export.

To make your own, see Create and manage themes.
