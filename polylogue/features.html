Polylogue

Features

A full Mastodon client, built with SwiftUI for iPhone, iPad and Mac, with translation at its centre.

Translation

Understand and be understood.

❝

Timeline translation

Posts in other languages are translated automatically, or only when you ask. Switch between original and translation at any time.

‡

On your device

Apple’s Translation framework is the default. Translations are cached, and you can clear the cache.

∞

Bridge through English

When there is no direct language pair, translate by way of English, with both steps on your device.

❡

Text in images

Open an image in a conversation to recognize its text, translate it, compare it with the detected text and copy it.

✦

Optional AI translation

Use OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or an OpenAI-compatible service with your own key, as a fallback or first.

✓

Language detection

Polylogue checks the post itself, so a wrongly labelled post is less likely to be missed.

Writing

Post with care.

❞

Reply across languages

Write in your language, review and edit the translation, then publish.

§

Shorten with AI

When an AI translation runs past 500 characters, ask the same model to tighten it.

❦

Photos and video

Attach up to four items from Photos or Files, each with an accessibility description.

≡

Polls

Two to four choices, single or multiple, from five minutes to seven days, with totals shown or hidden.

†

Audience and warnings

Public, Quiet Public or Followers, with content warnings. Replies keep the conversation’s privacy.

¶

Drafts and editing

Unfinished text is saved for new posts and for each reply. Edit or delete your own posts.

Reading and discovery

Find the conversation.

⌂

Three timelines

Home, Local and Federated, with automatic updates while open, refresh and endless scrolling.

❧

Full conversations

See a post with what came before it and every reply your server knows about.

✺

Explore

Trending posts, hashtags and news from your server, plus account suggestions.

⌕

Search

Find people, hashtags and posts, including people on other servers.

☆

Favorites, boosts, bookmarks

Synced with your server, with their own lists for what you saved and favorited.

◷

Notifications

Mentions, follows, favorites, boosts and finished polls, translated like the rest.

People and your account

Your space, your rules.

◉

Profiles

See an account’s details and recent posts, and follow from there.

⊘

Mute, block, report

Deal with unwanted accounts, and send reports to your moderators, optionally forwarding to theirs.

✎

Edit your profile

Name, biography, up to four profile fields, follower approval and discovery.

⚙

Server defaults

New posts start with the audience, language and warning settings saved on your server.

▣

Native everywhere

A sidebar layout on iPad and Mac that folds neatly onto iPhone, in light and dark.

✧

Demo account

Try the interface without a server. Nothing is published.
