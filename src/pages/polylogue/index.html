For iPhone, iPad and Mac

A social timeline without language barriers.

Polylogue is a native Mastodon app. Read posts in your own language, translated on your device, and reply in theirs after checking exactly what will be published.

The idea

Join the conversation, whatever language it is in.

A polylogue is a conversation among many. Mastodon already connects people around the world; Polylogue helps them understand one another.

❝

Read

Posts in other languages are translated as you scroll, using Apple’s Translation framework. The original is always one tap away.

❞

Reply

Write in your own language, preview the translation, edit it, and only then publish.

✦

Stay in control

AI translation is optional, uses your own key, and is never used for private or direct posts.

Translation

On your device first.

Apple’s Translation framework does the work on your device. Nothing about it passes through Pluria Labs.

  • Show original or show translation, post by post.
  • Translate through English when Apple has no direct pair, with both steps on your device.
  • Text in images is recognized on your device and translated.
  • Optional AI from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or a compatible service, with your own API key.

Languages and language pairs depend on what Apple supports on your device.

Reply across languages

  • Write your reply in your reading language.
  • Press Preview to see your original and the translation together.
  • Edit the translation until it says what you mean.
  • Press Publish. Only the version you reviewed is posted.

Mastodon publishes one language version of each post. Polylogue keeps your original on your device.

Privacy

Your conversations are none of our business.

Polylogue talks to your Mastodon server, not to a server of ours.

No Polylogue account

Sign in with the Mastodon account you already have. Your password is entered only on your server.

No tracking

No advertising, no analytics SDK and no telemetry service run by Pluria Labs.

Keys in the Keychain

Your sign-in token and any AI keys are kept in the Keychain, on that device only.

Private stays private

Followers-only and direct posts are never sent to an AI service.

Questions

Good to know.

What does Polylogue run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 18 or later, and Mac with macOS 15 or later.

Do I need a Mastodon account?

Yes, on Mastodon or a Mastodon-compatible server. To look around first, choose Explore with a demo account; nothing you do there is published.

Do I need an AI service?

No. Translation with Apple’s framework works without one. AI is a fallback you can turn on.

Is the app itself translated?

Not yet. Polylogue’s own menus and buttons are in English. It translates the posts you read and the replies you write.

Is Polylogue made by Mastodon?

No. Polylogue is an independent app by Pluria Labs and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Mastodon.

Where can I learn how to use it?

The User Guide covers signing in, translation, writing and your account, with search across the whole guide.
