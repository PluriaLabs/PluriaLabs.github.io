Before you report a bug

  • Search the open and closed issues.
  • Check that you are using the latest version of Polylogue.
  • See Troubleshoot common problems.
  • Issues on GitHub are public. Never post a password, an API key or a private post.

Common questions

Why is a post not translated?

Apple may not support that language pair on your device, or a language download may be waiting. Check Translate posts.

Who handles moderation and my account?

Your Mastodon server does. Pluria Labs cannot reset your password, remove posts or review reports.
