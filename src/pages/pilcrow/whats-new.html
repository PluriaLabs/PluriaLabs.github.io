Pilcrow

What’s new

Pilcrow has not had its first public release yet. This is what has been built recently.

November 2026

Book projects

Turn a folder into a book: order your chapters and scenes, track word counts against a target, and compile the manuscript into one Word, PDF or HTML document. Read the guide chapter.

On-device translation

Translate a document or a selection with Apple’s Translation framework, so the text stays on your device. Code, links and Markdown markers come back unchanged. Needs macOS 15 or iOS 18 or later. Read the guide chapter.

Mac-style menus on iPad

The iPad header now shows File, Edit, Insert, Format, View and Help beside the document’s name.

Compare and accept changes

Compare the open document with a saved version or another file, then take changes back or accept them one at a time. Read the guide chapter.

October 2026

Fill-in fields for templates

A macro’s blanks stay in the text as fields you can move between from the keyboard. Read the guide chapter.
